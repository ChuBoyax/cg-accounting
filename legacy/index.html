Welcome

Quality professional service, with personal attention for every client.

Established in 1991, CG Accounting is dedicated to the highest quality professional service and guidance, with personal attention given to all clients.

We combine technical knowledge, many years of experience, and an understanding of current issues and regulatory matters. As a multicultural and multilingual firm, from our office in Fort Lauderdale we serve clients throughout the United States, Israel, Europe, and Latin America.

We are committed to helping our clients with insightful advice, always available for quick responses, with the highest standard of excellence and professionalism.

Our story

From Wall Street to Fort Lauderdale.

Early years

Philadelphia, Israel, New York

David Goldis, third generation, was born in Philadelphia. His family moved to Israel for his first year of elementary school, and he was then brought up in Far Rockaway, NY, where he completed high school.

Jerusalem

First year of university abroad

His first year of university studies was on the Yeshiva University campus in Jerusalem, Israel.

1987

B.S. in Accounting

He returned to New York and earned his Bachelor of Science in Accounting from Yeshiva University in January 1987.

New York City

Spicer & Oppenheim

His professional life started at Spicer & Oppenheim, a well-known Wall Street firm that specialized in accounting for brokerage firms such as Bear Stearns.

The “Big Eight”

Peat Marwick and Coopers & Lybrand

He went on to work for two of the “Big Eight” firms: Peat Marwick, at the time the #1 accounting firm in the world, and Coopers & Lybrand.

1991

CG Accounting Corp. opens

David opened his own firm, CG Accounting Corp., now located in Fort Lauderdale, Florida.
